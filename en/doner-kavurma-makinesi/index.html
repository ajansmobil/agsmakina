Doner Roasting Machine

The Doner Roasting Machine offers high efficiency and quality in doner production. This machine ensures the homogeneous and perfect roasting of doner meats. Made from durable materials, it is long-lasting and suitable for intensive use. With a 0.75 KW motor, it can roast up to 250 kg of doner meat per hour. It is easy to use, simple to clean, and reduces operating costs. The doner roasting machine is an excellent choice for restaurants, doner shops, and large-scale production facilities.
